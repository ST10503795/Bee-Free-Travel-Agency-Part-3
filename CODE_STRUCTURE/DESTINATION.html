DISCOVER FRANCE

Paris — The City of Love

What makes Paris special?

Paris is known for romance, art, fashion, historic buildings and café culture. Visitors can explore picturesque streets, traditional bakeries and famous landmarks throughout the city.

What can you look forward to?

  • Visiting the Eiffel Tower.
  • Exploring the Louvre Museum.
  • Walking beside the Seine River.
  • Discovering French cafés and pastries.
  • Visiting beautiful historic neighbourhoods.

Accommodation in Paris

Paris offers boutique hotels, city apartments and luxury accommodation. Travellers interested in historic streets may enjoy exploring Le Marais, while those focused on sightseeing may prefer convenient public transport connections.

Things to know before visiting

  • Paris is the capital city of France.
  • The currency is the Euro (EUR).
  • French is the official language.
  • The Eiffel Tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair.

DISCOVER THE UAE

Dubai — A City of Innovation

What makes Dubai special?

Dubai combines striking modern buildings, luxury shopping, innovative developments and desert landscapes. Visitors can explore world-famous attractions while also discovering traditional markets and local culture.

What can you look forward to?

  • Visiting the Burj Khalifa.
  • Exploring Dubai Mall.
  • Enjoying a desert safari experience.
  • Discovering Palm Jumeirah.
  • Exploring waterfront areas and beaches.

Accommodation in Dubai

Dubai has luxury hotels, modern city accommodation and beachfront resorts. Areas such as Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina and Jumeirah offer different travel experiences depending on the visitor's interests.

Things to know before visiting

  • Dubai is in the United Arab Emirates.
  • The currency is the UAE Dirham (AED).
  • The Burj Khalifa is 828 metres tall.
  • Palm Jumeirah is a famous artificial island.

DISCOVER TANZANIA

Zanzibar — A Tropical Paradise

What makes Zanzibar special?

Zanzibar is a beautiful island destination off the coast of Tanzania. It is known for tropical beaches, clear ocean water, Swahili culture and the historic streets of Stone Town.

What can you look forward to?

  • Relaxing on white-sand beaches.
  • Exploring historic Stone Town.
  • Taking boat trips and snorkelling.
  • Visiting local spice plantations.
  • Enjoying Swahili food and culture.

Accommodation in Zanzibar

Zanzibar offers beachfront resorts, boutique hotels and smaller guesthouses. Travellers seeking relaxing beach settings often explore Nungwi and Kendwa, while Stone Town offers an experience closer to the island's history and culture.

Things to know before visiting

  • Zanzibar forms part of Tanzania.
  • It is located in the Indian Ocean.
  • The currency is the Tanzanian Shilling (TZS).
  • Swahili is widely spoken.
