A LUXURIOUS STAY

Luxury Hotel — Dubai

Experience comfort and elegance

Luxury hotels in Dubai can provide comfortable suites, modern interiors, attractive views and access to a variety of leisure facilities. They may be ideal for travellers who enjoy comfort during a busy city holiday.

After sightseeing, shopping or visiting local attractions, visitors can return to a relaxing space to rest and enjoy their stay.

Possible facilities and experiences

  • Comfortable rooms and suites.
  • Swimming pools and relaxation areas.
  • Restaurants and dining experiences.
  • Fitness centres and spa facilities.
  • Convenient access to city attractions.

Where can you find this style of accommodation?

Dubai features luxury accommodation in areas such as Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina and Jumeirah. Each area offers a different setting, including city attractions or waterfront surroundings.

Who might enjoy a luxury hotel?

Couples, families and travellers who appreciate comfortable accommodation and premium facilities may enjoy this type of stay.

Individual hotel facilities, pricing and availability vary and must be confirmed before booking.

A COASTAL ESCAPE

Beach Resort — Relaxing Retreat

Relax beside the ocean

Beachfront resorts can offer travellers a peaceful environment with ocean scenery, comfortable accommodation and places to enjoy time outdoors.

Depending on the property, guests may enjoy swimming pools, beachside dining, water activities and relaxing outdoor spaces.

Possible facilities and experiences

  • Beautiful beach and ocean views.
  • Swimming pools and relaxation spaces.
  • Restaurants with coastal scenery.
  • Boat trips and water activities.
  • Comfortable rooms and holiday facilities.

Where can you find beach resorts?

Beachfront resorts are found in many coastal destinations. Zanzibar, for example, is known for tropical beaches and island accommodation around areas such as Nungwi and Kendwa.

Who might enjoy a beach resort?

Couples, families and travellers who want a relaxing holiday by the ocean may enjoy this accommodation style.

Individual resort amenities, availability and prices must be checked separately.
